What Is a Good Score in Slope?

There is no universal good Slope score. Use personal-best ranges, consistency and run quality to set a better target.

⚡
Quick answer

There is no universal “good score” in Slope that applies to every build, leaderboard and date. A useful benchmark is one that is tied to the version you played and compared with your own recent runs.

Use your baseline first

Play several normal attempts and look at the range, not only the best run. A score that is consistently above your recent average is evidence of improvement even if it would not rank highly on an old public leaderboard.

External leaderboards need a date

A provider’s weekly, monthly or all-time board can change. If you cite a number, record when and where it was observed instead of presenting it as a permanent threshold.

Judge run quality with the score

A higher number can come from a favorable route as well as better control. Pair the result with a short cause note: how many emergency corrections occurred, whether landings stayed stable and what ended the run.

Set process targets

A goal such as “three runs without an oversteer crash” can be more actionable than an arbitrary number. Once the process becomes reliable, score improvement tends to follow naturally.
