Because those labels come from different contexts, this site presents them separately rather than declaring one source “wrong.”

Why attribution looks inconsistent online

Browser games often appear on portals, mirrors and later studio pages. “Made by,” “developer,” “publisher” and “hosted by” are different relationships. short summaries online frequently collapse them into one sentence; this guide does not.
