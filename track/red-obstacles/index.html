Slope Obstacles: Red Blocks & Collision Hazards

Red geometry is a lethal visual language in Slope: plan around it early rather than testing the edge of its collision space.

⚡
Quick answer

Slope obstacles that are marked in red should be treated as lethal hazards in the standard game. Create visible clearance early and plan the exit line so the dodge does not send the ball off the opposite edge.

Read the safe space around the block

Do not focus only on the obstacle. Compare the open width on both sides, your current lateral motion and the track that follows. The safest pass is the one with enough clearance and a usable exit.

Move before the collision feels urgent

Late avoidance requires more steering in less time. Start the lane change while the obstacle is still ahead, then release the key early enough for the ball to settle through the opening.

Avoid edge-to-edge dodges

Moving as far away from a red block as possible can create a new risk at the platform edge. Use only the clearance you need and preserve margin on the opposite side.

Do not assume an exact invisible hitbox

Different hosted builds may vary at the margins. Strategy should rely on obvious visible space rather than claiming that a near-graze is safe to a specific pixel or distance.
